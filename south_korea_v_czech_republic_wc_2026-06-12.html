🔑 3 Keys to the Match

1. SK's Physical Vulnerability
South Korea were demolished 4-0 by Ivory Coast and lost 1-0 to Austria in March friendlies — both physical, organised sides. Czech Republic with Soucek (6'4") and Schick are built exactly for this. SK's back-5 experiment under Hong Myung-Bo is a direct response to those beatings.

2. The BTTS No Streak
BTTS No has landed in 8 consecutive South Korea matches and 14 of their last 15. They're either keeping clean sheets against weaker Asian sides or getting shut out by stronger opposition. Czech Republic are short of elite goalscoring options beyond Schick and set-pieces.

3. Must-Not-Lose Dynamic
With Mexico heavily favoured to top Group A, this is effectively a playoff for 2nd place. Both managers know a draw keeps them alive — a loss is catastrophic. Expect both to set up cautiously, especially in the first 60 minutes. Hot/humid Guadalajara conditions + possible thunderstorms reinforce a slow tempo.

⚠️ Key Risks

1. Sportsbet correlation trap: "1 leg fails" promo — but if 2+ legs fail, you get NOTHING from SB. Our conservative legs are correlated. If the match opens up and becomes a goal-fest, BTTS No AND Under 2.5 both die together. This is the #1 risk.

2. Verify T&Cs: Does Sportsbet require exactly 1 leg to fail, or at least 1? Some promos only pay if precisely 1 leg misses. Check the fine print.

3. Bonus bet conversion: 70% assumed via Back/Lay arbitrage. Less liquid markets during live World Cup action may reduce this. Budget for 60-65% worst case.

4. SGM builder restrictions: Sportsbet and Neds may reject combos with correlated goals markets (BTTS No + U2.5). Have backup legs ready.
