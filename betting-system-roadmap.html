Post-World-Cup consolidation plan

One audited betting system.
Not two competing ones.

The World Cup pipeline proved the operating discipline. The betting engine contains useful probability, risk and ledger work. This roadmap keeps the best of both without rebuilding the stack or weakening the gates.

World Cup pipeline operatingConsolidation plannedArchitecture reviewed by GLM + SolLast updated · 14 July 2026

1The decision

Keep the pipeline as the control plane. Use the engine as a library.

The pipeline owns schedule locks, research, price-bound cards, independent review, gating and delivery. The engine contributes narrow, testable services: match-market probability, risk sizing, bankroll controls, settlement and performance reporting. No codebase merger. No blind model replacement.

2Honest starting point

What is working now done

  • World Cup workflow produces frozen research, odds snapshots, card JSON and review artifacts.
  • Cards are bound to real market and selection identifiers before delivery.
  • Independent review and structural gates can kill bad or fabricated legs.
  • The engine has football/NRL probability modules, risk concepts and a substantial test base.
  • GLM review + Sol Codex review agree: use a contract boundary, not a merger.

What is not ready yet gap

  • Engine and pipeline have no reliable shared card/ledger contract.
  • Two ledger model families are in conflict; current failures must be repaired first.
  • Match-result models do not validate player-prop legs.
  • Pipeline cards do not yet feed an idempotent settlement/performance loop.
  • MLB and NBA are future sport stubs, not live prediction systems.

5Non-negotiable guardrails

Evidence before bet

No research pack, price snapshot or market binding means no card. A model output is evidence, not permission.

Advisory before enforcement

New engine checks first report disagreement. They only become gates after calibration supports a threshold.

Lossless before migration

No ledger unification if fields vanish, IDs are unstable, or settlement cannot be replayed idempotently.

No drift during the tournament

World Cup delivery stays frozen while engine repairs and contracts are prepared separately.

One source of truth

Run artifacts preserve how a card was made; the canonical ledger preserves what was actually bet and what happened.

Sport-specific proof

MLB and NBA only progress when their data, model, market mapping and outcome feedback exist — not because a dispatcher has a stub.

6What this ultimately unlocks

🧾

Auditable decisions

Every delivered card can link its research, quoted price, review verdict, gate result, actual stake and final settlement.

📈

Learning loop

Outcome data can reveal which sports, markets, confidence tiers and card structures genuinely add value.

🧩

Multi-sport without chaos

One control plane and one ledger, with separate adapters and evidence standards for football, NRL, MLB and NBA.

7What done looks like

  • A single versioned ledger record survives legacy import/export without loss.
  • Each delivered card has stable card and leg identifiers.
  • Pipeline intake is idempotent and cannot overwrite a different run.
  • P7 records settled outcomes and supports performance reporting.
  • P3.5 reports supported-market model disagreement without pretending to price unsupported props.
  • NRL is the first fully extended post-WC lane; MLB/NBA remain evidence-gated future work.
