🎯 How the Betting Engine Works

Last updated · 2026-07-26 06:00 AEST

1The short version

The engine is a daily betting analyst and scorekeeper. It gathers the day's matches and prices, estimates how likely each outcome is, compares that estimate with the bookmaker's price, and either produces a carefully checked recommendation or says why there is no bet.

In parallel, it records the same picks as paper bets — pretend money under the same risk rules — then settles them after the event so you can see whether the model is genuinely accurate before risking cash.

It never presses the Place Bet button. Danny does that manually.

2A typical day

  1. Morning — collect the slate. Load today's fixtures, bookmaker prices, and the latest historical data.
  2. Analyse — price each outcome. The model estimates the chance of home/draw/away or another market. The engine compares that chance with the odds.
  3. Build the card. Candidates with enough apparent value are bound to the exact match, market, selection and price. Missing or stale data becomes BLOCKED, not a guess.
  4. Review. The card passes mechanical checks and an independent review. Danny sees the recommendation, price floor, stake and warnings.
  5. Paper bet by default. The engine records a pretend AUD 10 bet. No bookmaker account or real money is touched.
  6. Real bet only later. If every real-money gate is satisfied and Danny explicitly says GO, he places it manually.
  7. After the match — settle and learn. The result, return and closing price are recorded. Paper, cash and bonus performance stay separate.

3Paper vs Real

Paper — the flight simulator

Fake AUD 1,000 bankroll, fake stakes, same conservative limits as real.

No bookmaker placement. Recorded as approved_paper_offline.

Paper wins never top up the real bankroll. Paper losses never reduce it.

Real — actual cash

Real AUD 1,000 bankroll. Allowlisted personal Sportsbet or Bet365 account only.

Danny performs placement by hand. Reports never blend the two lanes.

4The pipeline — P-1 to P6

P-1Freeze the rules — record which rules, schedule and workflow this run will use so they cannot change halfway through.
P0Lock the run — give the day's run a fixed identity and timetable.
P1Capture the market — save the exact bookmaker events, markets, selections and odds so the analysis can be replayed.
P2Research the games — assemble source-attributed form, team news, weather and sport-specific checks.
P3Build candidates — turn model value into a card tied to exact prices and selections — never a vague "back Team A".
P4Independent review — verify every leg, the research, the price binding and the card structure.
P5Seven gates — run the final structural checks and issue a one-use approval token only if all pass.
P6Recheck immediately — confirm the market still exists and the price has not moved beyond tolerance.

5What the model predicts

For football, the core models learn team attack strength, defence strength and home advantage from historical scores, then estimate the chances of a home win, draw and away win.

The useful output is a probability — e.g. "our estimate is 55%". The bookmaker's odds imply its own probability. If the model's estimate is higher by enough to survive fees and uncertainty, the selection may become a candidate.

A probability is advice, not permission. The price, freshness, market identity, risk and human gates still have to pass.

6The 1/3/3/5 controls

Both lanes start from AUD 1,000. These are speed limits and an emergency brake — they favour survival and trustworthy learning over fast growth.

1%
Per bet — AUD 10 max

3%
Per day — AUD 30 max
A bad slate can't create a large daily loss
3%
Open at once — AUD 30 max
Unsettled bets can't quietly stack up
5%
Drawdown stop — AUD 50
Betting stops; the engine doesn't chase losses
5%
Odds drift tolerance
Price deteriorates beyond 5%? Bet is cancelled

7Where the odds come from

The Odds API — 500 credits, 68 sports, 31 bookmakers. Fast, structured, quota-aware. Used for consensus pricing and broad market coverage.

Scrape-facade — a Sportsbet-only, read-only browser route. Opens a Sportsbet event, extracts visible markets and prices. Separate cache from the Odds API so the two sources can be compared or used as fallbacks without pretending they're the same evidence.

8What's missing for the first real bet

  • Connect a calibrated model probability to a fresh Sportsbet selection — without it, edge cannot be calculated
  • Complete one successful fresh-event capture and pass it through the full evaluator
  • Calculate same-day exposure, open exposure and drawdown from the real ledger (not typed-in zeroes)
  • Freeze one exact real candidate with market, selection, price, model probability, positive edge, stake ≤ AUD 10
  • Sol reviews that exact candidate packet
  • Danny records an explicit GO or NO-GO

Until all of these are complete, the right output is paper recommendations and accuracy tracking — not a real-money bet.

9The rhythm

Recommend in the morning → Track a paper position → Settle later → Improve from the evidence
