Phase 1 — two overlapping bets

Place $50 on each of two overlapping even-money bets. Suitable pairs include:

  • Red + High
  • Black + Low
  • Red + Even
  • Black + Odd

Do not use opposing pairs such as Red + Black, High + Low, or Odd + Even. Those cannot produce a 2/2 result.

On a single-zero wheel with a suitable pair: 2/2 = 9/37, 1/2 = 18/37, and 0/2 = 10/37. Keep the pair fixed; do not rotate based on the recent board.

Profit ladder — two dozens or columns

After a 2/2 Phase 1 result, risk only the current ladder profit. Place the listed total across two dozens or two columns. A win covers 24 of 37 numbers and produces a net profit equal to half the total stake.

Successful run

One complete ladder follows this sequence:

  1. Phase 1 returns 2/2: $500 → $600.
  2. Phase 2 wins: $600 → $650.
  3. Phase 3 wins: $650 → $725.
  4. Phase 4 wins: $725 → $837.50.
  5. Phase 5 wins: $837.50 → $1,006.25.
  • Win the completed ladder: stop immediately at $1,006.25.
  • Lose a profit leg: return to Phase 1 with the $500 base.

Operating rules

  1. Keep the $500 starting base protected once Phase 1 produces the first $100 profit.
  2. Do not increase the next stake to recover a loss.
  3. Do not recycle a completed ladder.
  4. Confirm the terminal's displayed minimum, maximum, and aggregate wager limits before playing.
