6What to push back on before signing

Six clauses worth tightening. None are deal-breakers, but each materially affects TCO.

  1. 1
    3-month term → Negotiate 12 months (industry standard) or 6-month lock + 6-month review
    TGE is hedging on fuel/labour. Industry standard is 12-month fixed.
  2. 2
    Quarterly rate reviews → Add CPI cap clause (max +X% per review)
    No upward cap in the doc. Aug 2026 review is the obvious jump point.
  3. 3
    $200/month minimum → Clarify behaviour if trade dips below the floor
    Does the rate card get re-rated upward? Invoiced top-up? Confirm in writing.
  4. 4
    Fuel surcharge undefined → Get current FSC % + the index it tracks in writing
    FSC is added on top. Market is 20-25% — some carriers sneak in 28-30%.
  5. 5
    Reciprocal = N on every lane → Ask to flip Reciprocal to Y for symmetric trade lanes
    Unlocks 10-20% lower per-kg on return lanes. Material if Montu moves freight both ways.
  6. 6
    Account No. is 0 → Confirm real account number on the DocuSign; ask to waive the $37.50 Account Service Fee
    Doc is currently a template. Final sign needs correct number, legal entity, and fee waiver.
  7. 7
    No ADL or PER origins → Add to this card or get a separate amendment for SA/WA origins
    If you ship from South Australia or Western Australia, the card is incomplete.
  8. 8
    EXPRESS only — no Economy tier → Ask for an Economy/Standard tier for non-urgent freight
    Likely 15-25% cheaper per kg on non-time-critical lanes.
