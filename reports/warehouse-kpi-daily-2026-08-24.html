Methodology

Hours: activity-session inference from distinct same-day timestamps. Consecutive observed events are connected only up to a 60-minute gap; each observed gap is capped at 60 minutes. A single event has no supported denominator and displays —. These are observed activity hours, not clocked attendance, payroll, or performance determination.

APH: (Picked + Packed + Received × 0.25) ÷ inferred hours. Receiving quantities are canonical allocations counted once across distinct receivers; no latest-user proxy or reducer-run filter is used.

Warehouse normalization and fallback: only QLD/NSW/VIC are rendered. An explicit source warehouse takes precedence; WMS/Change/unrecognized values use the approved employee mapping at /home/daz/knowledge-vault/raw/warehouse-kpis/app/config/employee_warehouse.csv.
