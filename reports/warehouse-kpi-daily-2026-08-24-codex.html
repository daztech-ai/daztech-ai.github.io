Definitions

Methodology

Rates and hours

Pick/hr, Pack/hr and Receive/hr use timestamps from that activity stream only. APH uses all distinct activity timestamps and weights receiving at 0.25: (picked + packed + received × 0.25) ÷ observed hours.

Observed hours sum consecutive distinct event gaps and cap each gap at 60 minutes. A single timestamp cannot support a denominator and displays —.

Attribution

Explicit QLD/NSW/VIC source values take precedence. Generic WMS/Change values use the reviewed employee mapping. “Rechard Oozeer” is assigned to QLD from repeated Cin7 Brisbane warehouse title evidence and remains marked roster pending.

This is directional operational reporting. It is not clocked attendance, payroll evidence, or an automatic performance determination.
