2B field brief · Perth site visit · 11 August 2026

Health House International
WA fulfilment expansion

Medicinal-cannabis warehousing, Schedule 8 security and Western Australian fulfilment.

Decision rule: Do not assume the existing premises, vault, permits or licences transfer to a new operator. Verify the exact legal entity, address, approvals, stock scope and transfer/new-application requirements with WA Health before valuing the opportunity.

1. Executive brief

Opportunity

A Perth-based fulfilment hub could reduce east-coast freight exposure and improve replenishment times for WA pharmacies and patients, particularly regional WA.

Main constraint

For a wholesaler/distributor storing S8 medicines, WA Health guidance points to a Grade VII AS 3809:1998 strongroom—not merely a pharmacy drug safe.

Biggest diligence risk

Product-specific TGA approvals, stock availability, prescription errors and non-substitution rules can delay fulfilment even when the warehouse is operational.

3. WA medicinal-cannabis regulatory map

Classification

Medicinal cannabis may be S4 or S8. Cannabis formulations, dronabinol, nabilone, nabiximols and CBD products below the relevant 98% threshold are generally S8 under the WA pharmacist guide.

Supply pathways

Many products are not ARTG-registered and use the TGA Special Access Scheme or Authorised Prescriber pathway. Wholesalers may require SAS/AP evidence before release.

Pharmacy dispensing

Valid prescriptions can be presented to any WA pharmacy. Pharmacists must authenticate the prescription, confirm identity and prescriber bona fides, and review ScriptCheckWA as appropriate.

Substitution

Product/brand, formulation and cannabinoid strength must match the prescription. A non-equivalent substitute generally requires prescriber authorisation and a new prescription.

Interstate prescriptions

The July 2025 WA guide states that valid interstate medicinal-cannabis prescriptions may be dispensed in WA if they contain the information required by WA law.

Other approvals

Check TGA requirements and, where applicable, Office of Drug Control approvals for importing, manufacturing or supplying controlled medicinal-cannabis products.

4. S8 storage: what the site must demonstrate

Use caseWA guidance
Wholesaler / distributorStrongroom meeting Resistance Grade VII of AS 3809:1998.
PharmacyLarge safe in a non-public area, covered by a continuously monitored detection device.
Small safeUp to 500 human doses, subject to the small-safe requirements; above 250 doses, continuously monitored movement detectors are required.
Large safeMore than 500 human doses; continuously monitored detectors. Pharmacy sites always require a large safe.

Small/large safe specifications from WA Health

  • Small safe: AS 3809 Grade I or Schedule 3 specifications; solid steel/concrete construction, continuous welding, secure hinge design and equivalent high-security lock.
  • Small safe must be directly secured to concrete floor/wall or suitable structural members—not simply to a stand, shelf or plinth.
  • Large safe: AS 3809 Grade II or Schedule 3 specifications; minimum 250 kg, at least two locking bolts, direct concrete-floor mounting and licensed safe installation.
  • These safe specifications do not replace the wholesaler strongroom requirement.

Inspect and request evidence

  • AS 3809:1998 Grade VII certification/design and completion certificate.
  • Licensed installer details and any engineering/fire/building approvals.
  • Continuously monitored alarm, detector coverage, monitoring contract and response SLA.
  • CCTV coverage, access-control logs, key/card governance and visitor procedures.
  • Strongroom capacity, maximum approved stock and segregation of S4/S8/returns/quarantine.
  • Current WA Health licences/permits tied to the exact address and legal entity.

5. Fulfilment timing — WA planning assumptions

WA does not publish a reliable average pharmacy-to-patient fulfilment time. The ranges below are operational planning assumptions, not regulatory standards or HHI performance claims.

ScenarioPlanning range
Perth pharmacy has stockSame day to 1 business day
Perth pharmacy orders from a local WA wholesaler1–3 business days
Perth pharmacy orders from an eastern-states warehouse3–7 business days
Regional WA deliveryUsually 3–7 business days
National stockout / special order1–3+ weeks

A Perth hub may realistically remove roughly 1–3 business days of east-coast freight time, but it will not remove TGA-approval, prescription, stockout or pharmacy-dispensing delays.

Ask HHI for measured data

  • Median pharmacy order-to-dispatch time.
  • Perth metro versus regional WA delivery time.
  • Percentage of WA orders fulfilled from WA-held inventory.
  • Stockout rate and average back-order duration.
  • Same-day dispatch cut-off and carrier performance.
  • Percentage of delays caused by missing SAS/AP documentation or prescription issues.

6. Site-visit question list

  1. Who owns and operates the premises today?
  2. Which entity holds every WA and Commonwealth approval?
  3. Are approvals site-specific, transferable, or subject to a new application?
  4. Can we see the Grade VII strongroom certificate and alarm-monitoring evidence?
  5. What is the maximum approved S8 stock volume and current utilisation?
  6. What products are S4 versus S8?
  7. What are the inbound receiving, quarantine, release, picking, packing and returns controls?
  8. How are discrepancies, destruction, recalls and temperature excursions handled?
  9. What WMS, batch/expiry, serialised or controlled-drug audit trail is in use?
  10. What are the actual 12-month service levels for pharmacies and patients?
  11. Which leases, equipment, staff, SOPs, contracts and licences are included in the proposed transaction?
  12. Has WA Health inspected the premises recently, and are there open corrective actions?
