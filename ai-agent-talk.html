Don’t ask: “What can AI say?”

Start with the outcome. Give it the context. Let it use the right tools. Keep a human at the important decisions.

A prompt is a brief

Good prompts are not magic.
They are clear handovers.

ROLE You are my operations analyst.

GOAL Produce a 5-minute daily brief for a GM.

CONTEXT Use the attached metrics and yesterday’s actions.

RULES Separate facts from inference. Flag missing data. Do not invent numbers.

OUTPUT 3 headlines · risks · decisions needed · actions with owners.

AGI, without the hype

AGI means general intelligence,
not a smarter chatbot.

There is no single agreed definition. The useful version: systems that can perform across many domains, at high capability, with increasing independence.

GeneralityWorks across different kinds of problems.
CapabilityPerforms well, not merely plausibly.
AutonomyCan pursue goals over multiple steps.
Source: Levels of AGI for Operationalizing Progress · framing, not prediction
How to be ready

Don’t predict the future.
Build the muscles.

1 · DecomposeTurn big work into clear outcomes and steps.
2 · DocumentMake processes, definitions and decisions legible.
3 · VerifyKnow the source, the standard and the human owner.
4 · ExperimentRun small, safe trials on real recurring work.
5 · ShareKeep the best prompts and workflows reusable.
6 · Stay accountableAutomation never transfers responsibility.
The rule

Automate the repeatable.
Elevate the human.

Use AI for synthesis, first drafts, pattern finding and preparation. Keep judgement, relationships, sensitive decisions and accountability with people.

“If the work happens every week, write the workflow once.”

Your first experiment

Pick one task you do
every week.

TodayDescribe the outcome and current process.
This weekGive AI context, rules and a test dataset.
Next weekCompare quality, time and failure modes.
ThenImprove the workflow. Share the pattern.

Start small. Make it useful. Make it safe.

Takeaway

AI will not replace the team
that learns to work with it.

The advantage is not knowing the perfect tool.
It is knowing how to turn work into a clear, verifiable system.
