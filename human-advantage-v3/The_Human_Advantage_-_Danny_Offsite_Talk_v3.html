The formula operations inherited

Do we wait for success before we allow ourselves to feel proud?

Backlog clears → KPI improves → then we breathe
→
Notice progress → create energy → improve the next handoff

Operations never runs out of next goals. We need energy before the finish line—not only after it.

Adapt Achor's formula. If pride is always waiting behind the next target, operations never arrives. Reverse it: recognition and connection give people more capacity for the next improvement.

Not “everything is fine.” “This matters—and we can take the next useful action.”
Accurate means seeing risk and patient impact clearly. Agentic means finding what can move, who owns it and which next action helps. That is practical optimism in a control tower.

The 3–1–1 practice

3 things that moved
1 person thanked
1 friction made smaller
Three, one, one: attention, connection and action. Not three perfect outcomes—three signs of progress. Thank one person specifically. Remove or clarify one small friction.
