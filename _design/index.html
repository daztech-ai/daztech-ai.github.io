Card Design Mockups

Three mobile-first design-system prototypes for the Daz Betting Engine cards. Same CSS tokens, three different shapes. Click any card to open the live mockup.

Three measurable wins on every card

  1. Edge % visible without scrolling on mobile — 28px mono number, top-right, color-coded by tier (Unabated pattern).
  2. Lean readable in <1s glance — colored pill (LOCK / BACK / WATCH / FADE), not prose.
  3. 3-4 fixtures fit one mobile screen — stacked cards replace 7-column tables.

Lock1. NRL fixture card

Three fixtures in one screen: lock / watch / fade. Tier-colored left border, big edge numbers, best-odds green buttons, bankroll bar footer.

9.9KB · 1-col mobile, 2-col tablet, max-720 desktop

Back2. SGM card

3-leg same-game multi. Per-leg edge % and pill, combined-odds summary card with gold border, EV + suggested stake. Alt fade build on the same fixture for tier contrast.

10.0KB · single match card, legs + summary

Watch3. My Offers card

Bonus-bet offers sorted by EV. Top chips (active / bonus-bet / EV-positive / best EV). Per offer: pill, title, qualifying bet, EV number, ROI line, collapsible terms.

10.5KB · 5 offers, 4 tiers shown
