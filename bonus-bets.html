How the math works

For a 4-leg multi with each leg at true probability p:
P(win) = p4  ·  P(exactly 1 fail) = 4 × (1-p) × p3  ·  P(2+ fail) = 1 - P(win) - P(exactly 1 fail)

EV per $1 staked = P(win) × C + P(1-fail) × bonus_conversion − 1
where C = combined odds and bonus_conversion ≈ 0.75 (typical tight-market conversion).

Stake is capped at 5% of bankroll (Nutz's edge threshold). Even with no per-leg edge, the bonus structure alone often produces +EV at standard racing odds. View source
